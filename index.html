05 — Presentation

Staging & Showing Recommendations

Four moves to support the $859,900 list price when buyers walk through the door.

Show virtual staging options

Provide virtually staged alternatives for the dining room — a welcoming sitting lounge, reading nook, or home office den — to help buyers see past the front-entry layout without any physical re-staging.

Highlight the garage

Emphasize the oversized 2.5-car depth and width as functional workshop space or storage for lawn and recreational equipment.

Lead with outdoor living

Focus main marketing imagery on the inground pool, covered deck, and fenced yard — the home’s strongest differentiator this year.

Provide floor plans

Include a clear 2D floor plan in marketing materials so buyers understand the layout before viewing in person.
